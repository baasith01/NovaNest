NovaNest Core Problems:

Problem 1: Customer Retention
Business Situation:

Many customers order once but never return.

Business Impact:

High customer acquisition costs.

Goal:

Increase repeat customers.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Problem 2: Delivery Delays
Business Situation:

Customers complain during peak hours.

Business Impact:

Bad ratings and cancellations.

Goal:

Reduce delivery time.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Problem 3: Discount Dependency
Business Situation:

Customers only order when discounts are available.

Business Impact:

Profitability decreases.

Goal:

Find sustainable growth.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Problem 4: Restaurant Performance Gap
Business Situation:

Some restaurants perform poorly.

Business Impact:

Lower customer satisfaction.

Goal:

Identify top and weak performers.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Problem 5: Product Funnel Drop-offs
Business Situation:

Many users browse but don't purchase.

Business Impact:

Lost revenue opportunities.

Goal:

Increase conversions.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Problem 6: City Expansion Strategy
Business Situation:

Resources are limited.

Business Impact:

Wrong expansion decisions waste money.

Goal:

Find high-growth cities.
