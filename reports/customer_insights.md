# Customer Intelligence Insights

## Dataset Summary

Total Customers: 5000

## 1. City Distribution Analysis

### Observation

Chennai has the highest customer base with 1537 customers (30.7%).

### Insight

Metro cities dominate food delivery demand.

### Recommendation

Increase marketing investments and rider allocation in Chennai and Bangalore since they contribute the majority of customers.

---

## 2. Customer Persona Analysis

### Observation

Students, Working Professionals and Families are almost equally distributed.

Student: 1674

Working Professional: 1666

Family: 1660

### Insight

NovaNest currently has a balanced customer portfolio and is not dependent on a single segment.

### Recommendation

Create personalized campaigns for each segment instead of one common strategy.

---

## 3. Subscription Analysis

### Observation

Premium: 2524

Free: 2476

The distribution is almost 50-50.

### Insight

There is strong potential to convert free users into premium users.

### Recommendation

Introduce premium benefits such as free delivery and exclusive offers.

---

## 4. Customer Acquisition Analysis

### Observation

Instagram is the strongest channel.

Referral is the weakest channel.

### Insight

Digital marketing channels are driving customer acquisition.

### Recommendation

Increase investment in Instagram campaigns and improve referral programs.

---

## 5. Customer Growth Analysis

### Observation

Customer growth is stable throughout the months.

No major spikes or crashes exist.

### Insight

The business currently has healthy and sustainable growth.

### Recommendation

Focus on accelerating growth during low-performing months.
