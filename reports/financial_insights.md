# Financial Intelligence Insights

## Dataset Summary

Total Orders: 49,971

---

## 1. Revenue Analysis

### Observation

Total Revenue Generated: ₹48,281,651

### Insight

NovaNest has a strong transaction ecosystem capable of generating significant revenue.

### Recommendation

Focus on increasing repeat customer purchases instead of only customer acquisition.

---

## 2. Average Order Value Analysis

### Observation

Average Order Value: ₹966.19

### Insight

Customers have relatively high spending behavior.

### Recommendation

Introduce combo meals and premium bundles to further increase AOV.

---

## 3. Discount Analysis

### Observation

Total Discounts Given: ₹2,167,248

Average Discount per Order: ₹43.37

### Insight

Discount dependency is currently moderate.

### Recommendation

Shift from blanket discounts to personalized promotions.

---

## 4. Delivery Fee Analysis

### Observation

Total Delivery Fees Collected: ₹1,996,063

### Insight

Delivery fees contribute a secondary revenue stream.

### Recommendation

Introduce dynamic pricing during peak demand periods.

---

## 5. Business Strategy Summary

Primary Revenue Driver: Orders

Secondary Revenue Driver: Delivery Fees

Major Cost Driver: Discounts

Growth Opportunity: Premium customers and bundled offers
