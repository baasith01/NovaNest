# Operations Intelligence Insights

## Dataset Summary

Total Orders: 49,971

Delivered Orders: 47,994

Cancelled Orders: 1,977

Cancellation Rate: 3.96%

---

## 1. Order Status Analysis

### Observation

96% of orders are successfully delivered.

4% are cancelled.

### Insight

NovaNest currently has a healthy operational success rate.

### Recommendation

Investigate cancellation reasons to reduce customer churn.

---

## 2. Peak Hour Analysis

### Observation

Orders are distributed almost equally throughout the day.

### Insight

The current simulation does not yet represent realistic peak-hour behavior.

### Recommendation

In future iterations, implement stronger persona-based ordering patterns.

---

## 3. Monthly Order Analysis

### Observation

May has the highest order volume.

February has the lowest order volume.

### Insight

Seasonality exists in customer ordering behavior.

### Recommendation

Increase promotional campaigns during weaker months.

---

## 4. Delivery Time Analysis

### Observation

Average delivery duration is 37.38 minutes.

### Insight

This falls within an acceptable range for food delivery services.

### Recommendation

Target a delivery time below 35 minutes.

---

## 5. Delivery Distance Analysis

### Observation

Average delivery distance is 6.46 km.

### Insight

Delivery coverage is healthy.

### Recommendation

Optimize rider allocation to reduce travel distances.
